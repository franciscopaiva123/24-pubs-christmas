24 Pubs of Christmas
🎄24 Pubs of Christmas 2025🎄

General Rules

  • Start at the first pub and finish at the last. No skipping allowed.
  • One drink minimum at each location. Pace yourself wisely. (Or not)
  • Look out for your mates. Nobody gets left behind.
  • Take photos, make memories, but live in the moment.
  • Everytime you see a "monhe" get a beer.
  • Cash is king. Bring cash.
  • If you break a rule... Chugg a drink.
  • What happens on the crawl, stays on the crawl. Cheers!

Bar Rules - One for each bar.

  • Lefties Bar: Only drink with the left hand.
  • Accent Bar: Speak with a different accent.
  • Get-a-round Bar: Choose a friend and buy him/her a drink.
  • Rock-Paper-Scissors Bar: Play Rock-Paper-Scissors with a friend. If you lose, get the round.
  • Random Card Bar: In this bar the bartender chooses a random card to pay the beers. (4 people max)
  • Penalty Bar: Choose a friend the last to chugg a beer, buys two beers.
  • No Beer Bar: Self explanatory...
